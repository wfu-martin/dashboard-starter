# Team roles

Everyone builds, everyone tests. A role is the person who makes sure a part
of the project gets done well, not the only person who works on it. Teams of
four combine two roles.

| Role | Owns |
|---|---|
| Delivery Lead | The repository and its collaborators, merging, keeping the roadmap moving |
| Spec Lead | The stakeholder brief, the framed questions, `spec.md`, and its acceptance criteria |
| Data Lead | The data layer and the reconciliation tests: every number on screen is proven |
| Design Lead | Chart choice, layout, labeling, and the headline takeaway on every screen |
| Pitch Lead | The Demo Day pitch and the "The stakeholder asked... → We framed it as..." slide |
